FAQ

Before you
trust the radar.

Is this just a changelog aggregator?+

No. The product is organized around actionability and evidence: changes are classified as action required, watch, informational, or noise, then filtered to your selected stack.

Does Stack Safety scan my source code?+

Not in the founding pilot. It monitors the dependencies and services you select. A vendor change may be relevant to your stack without proving a specific line of your code is affected.

Where does the information come from?+

Official changelogs, release notes, security advisories and deprecation notices are the core evidence layer. Wider web and hands-on sources are used as secondary context, not as substitutes for primary evidence.

How often would I hear from it?+

The founding pilot is designed around weekly or twice-weekly personalized digests, with critical changes able to surface separately when appropriate.

Why start with email instead of a big dashboard?+

Because the first thing to prove is whether the signal is valuable. Email makes that test immediate; deeper monitoring, history and code-aware impact analysis can follow once users actually depend on it.
