FAQ

Before you
trust the radar.

Is this just a changelog aggregator?+

No. The product is designed around actionability: source-backed changes are classified by whether they require action, deserve watching, are merely informational, or are noise.

Does Stack Safety scan my source code?+

Not in the current product. It monitors the dependencies and services you select. A vendor change may be relevant to your stack without proving that a specific line of your code is affected.

Where does the information come from?+

Official changelogs, release notes, security advisories, deprecation notices and other primary vendor sources are the core evidence layer.

How often would I hear from it?+

The early product is designed for weekly or twice-weekly personalized digests, with critical changes able to surface separately.
